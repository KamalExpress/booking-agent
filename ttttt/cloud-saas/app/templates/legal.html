Software as a Service (SaaS) Terms of Service

Governing multi-tenant platform usage, worker coordination, and tenant responsibilities.

1. Provision of Services

This SaaS platform provides distributed orchestrator coordination, slot availability monitoring, and automated booking task management for registered agencies ("Tenants") and their authorized staff users.

2. Acceptable Use & Account Responsibility

Tenants are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials and ensuring that all applicant data submitted complies with applicable visa authority requirements. Un-authorized distribution of worker node tokens or tampering with system API endpoints is strictly prohibited.

3. Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Worker Availability

While worker nodes utilize high-resilience browser fingerprints to monitor slot availability continuously, slot availability depends on third-party visa portal schedules. The platform does not guarantee third-party portal slot drop frequencies.

Privacy Policy & Data Isolation Guarantee

How applicant data and tenant information are collected, processed, and secured.

1. Multi-Tenant Data Isolation

Our architecture enforces strict database row-level tenant boundary isolation. Applicant records, passport details, phone numbers, and booking histories registered under your agency tenant ID are accessible exclusively to authorized staff within your agency.

2. Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

The platform collects applicant details (Name, Passport Number, Passport Expiry, Contact Phone, and Email) solely for the purpose of executing automated visa appointment bookings requested by your agency staff.

3. Data Encryption Standards

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Portal account credentials and sensitive secret tokens are stored using AES-256 encryption at rest.

{% elif current_tab == 'cookies' %}

Cookie & Local Storage Disclosure

Understanding session cookies, Service Worker storage, and PWA Web Push notification tokens.

1. Essential Session Cookies

We use essential HTTP-only cookies (`access_token`) to maintain secure staff authentication sessions across dashboard navigation. These cookies contain no plaintext passwords.

2. Web Push Notification Tokens

When staff enable real-time slot alerts, Progressive Web App (PWA) push notification tokens are registered with the browser Service Worker. Tokens are used exclusively to deliver slot availability push alerts to your device.

{% elif current_tab == 'security' %}

Security, Audit Logging & Retention Policy

Immutable audit trails, administrative traceability, and data lifecycle rules.

1. Audit Trail Traceability

Administrative operations—including slot archiving, worker node state toggles, and client profile updates—are logged to an immutable `AuditLog` capturing the user ID, timestamp, and IP address.

2. Automated Log Purging

Transient network logs and worker status heartbeats are automatically purged after 30 days to optimize system memory and minimize unnecessary PII retention.
