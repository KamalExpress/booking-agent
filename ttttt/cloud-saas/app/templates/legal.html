Software as a Service (SaaS) Terms of Service

Governing platform usage, Digital Booking Specialist assistance, and agency responsibilities.

1. Provision of Platform Services

This platform provides workflow facilitation tools, appointment drop tracking, and in-system Digital Specialists to assist registered travel agencies ("Tenants") and their authorized staff in securing visa appointments efficiently on behalf of their clients.

2. Agency Responsibility & Client Authorization

Tenants are responsible for ensuring that all client records submitted to the platform are accurate, authorized by the applicant, and compliant with relevant visa authority guidelines.

3. Appointment Placement & Service Availability

Our in-system Digital Specialists work diligently to match queued applicants with appointment openings. Slot availability is dependent on official Visa Center release schedules.
