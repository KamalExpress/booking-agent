1. Client Directory & Applicant Profiles

Manage applicant records, passport info, and travel preferences.

1

Register Visa Applicants

Add applicant first name, surname, date of birth, gender, nationality, passport number, and expiry date via the Client Directory.

2

Strict Tenant Isolation

All applicant data added by your staff is strictly isolated to your agency tenant and never shared across agencies.

3

Quick Enqueue Action

Click the Enqueue button beside any client to instantly add them to the automated slot booking queue.

Pro-Tip for Staff

Ensure passport expiration dates are accurate prior to enqueuing. The automated booking engine checks passport validity against target appointment dates to prevent portal rejections.

2. Waitlist Queue & Automated Booking Tasks

Prioritize applicants and match them automatically with discovered slot drops.

1

Automated Matching Engine

When headless workers discover a slot drop at Lahore or Islamabad, queued applicants are automatically matched based on target date criteria.

2

Standard Appointment Codes

The platform defaults to GVC appointment type 26 (Long-Term Type D Seasonal/Dependent Employment) for maximum booking success rates.

Queue Priority Rules
  • First-In, First-Out (FIFO) queue ordering per Visa Center.
  • Automatic retry up to 3 attempts per booking task in case of OTP delays.
  • Instant status updates delivered to your agency inbox.

3. Live Slot Drop Tracking & Web Push Alerts

Receive real-time notifications on mobile & desktop the moment slots open.

AVAILABLE Status

Indicates verified appointment drops detected in real time by scanning workers.

VERIFYING Status

System is actively testing interval validation or completing a booking sequence.

UNAVAILABLE Status

Slots were claimed or interval expired. Worker nodes continue continuous monitoring.
